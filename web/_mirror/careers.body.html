Careers

​At Safe Harbours, we are passionate about creating safe, nurturing homes for children in need. If you’re committed to making a positive impact in the lives of children and families, we invite you to join our team.

In College?

We work with a number of colleges across Ontario to help students in child and youth care field complete their diplomas. Let us know if you would like to do your student placement with us at Safe Harbours.

Get in Touch

Why Work with Safe Harbours?

We offer a range of positions, from full-time roles to flexible, part-time options. Whether you’re looking to work directly with foster families or support our team behind the scenes, there’s a place for you at Safe Harbours.

Meaningful Impact Working with Safe Harbours means being part of a team that changes lives. Every day, you’ll have the chance to make a direct difference for children facing significant challenges.
Collaborative Environment Our team is built on trust, compassion, and support. We believe in working together to ensure the best outcomes for children, foster parents, and staff.
Growth and Development Safe Harbours provides ongoing training and professional development opportunities, ensuring that every team member has the skills and knowledge to succeed in their role.
