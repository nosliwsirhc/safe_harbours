## What Is a Foster Agency and What Does It Do?

A foster agency is responsible for recruiting, training, and supporting foster parents while ensuring children and youth placed in care receive safe, stable, and appropriate homes. In Ontario, foster agencies work closely with Children's Aid Societies to match children with foster families who can meet their specific needs, whether those needs are medical, emotional, behavioural, or developmental.

Beyond placement, foster agencies provide ongoing oversight and hands-on support. This typically includes training, regular home visits, 24/7 on-call assistance, and coordination with schools, therapists, and community services. As a foster parent, your agency becomes your primary source of guidance and advocacy, helping you navigate challenges while focusing on the day-to-day care of the child in your home.

## How Do You Sign Up to Become a Foster Parent in Mississauga?

Becoming a foster parent in Mississauga starts with choosing a licensed foster care agency and reaching out to begin the intake process. Once you connect with an agency, you can expect an initial conversation to discuss your interest, availability, and the types of placements you may be open to. This step helps ensure there is a good fit between you, the agency, and the needs of children currently requiring care.

From there, the process typically includes an application, background checks, a home study, and mandatory training. Training covers topics such as child development, trauma, behaviour support, and the day-to-day realities of fostering. Throughout the process, agencies assess not just your home, but your readiness, support system, and capacity to provide a safe and stable environment. While the steps require time and commitment, the goal is to prepare you fully before a child is placed in your care.
